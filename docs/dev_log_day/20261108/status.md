## 2026-11-10 — density between the cities

The world city heat map and the nine regional maps now draw population density between the
dots: suburbs and countryside from GHSL GHS-POP R2023A (2025, 30″ ≈ 1 km, CC BY 4.0),
condensed by `scripts/vendor_population_grid.py` into a 1 MB pyramid (0.1° / 0.25° / 0.5°,
uint16 people/km², sha256-pinned). New pure-Python `maps/raster.py` (bilinear sampling, level
choice by pixel size, PNG encoder) and inverse projections; the surface is one embedded PNG
clipped to the region's countries. Grey < 10/km², blue → red log ramp, red ≥ 2,000/km².
